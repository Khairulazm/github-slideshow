# AI Usage Policy (Template)

## Purpose
Enable safe, effective use of AI tools while protecting people and data.

## Permitted use
- Drafting, summarizing, translation, ideation
- Data‑free practice with public examples
- Approved tools for work content

## Prohibited use
- Sharing confidential, personal, or regulated data in unapproved tools
- Automated decisions without human review
- Misrepresentation or undisclosed AI authorship where prohibited

## Data handling
- Use redaction/anonymization where possible
- Store outputs in approved systems
- Retention per company policy

## Disclosure
- Disclose AI assistance when required by stakeholders or policy

## Review
- Submit high‑impact prompts/flows for review
- Maintain logs for audits
