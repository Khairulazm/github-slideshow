# Facilitator Guide

## Before
- Confirm objectives and audience roles
- Provision tools and accounts
- Share pre‑reads and setup instructions
- Prepare slides, labs, and breakout prompts

## During
- Set norms: cameras, participation, hands‑on
- Timebox segments and stick to agenda
- Demo slowly; narrate decision points
- Rotate support in breakouts
- Capture questions and parking lot

## After
- Send recap, materials, and recordings
- Collect feedback survey
- Review capstone proposals and give quick wins
- Schedule office hours
