# Glossary (Beginner)

- Model: The AI system that generates outputs from inputs
- Prompt: The instruction or question you give the model
- Context window: How much text the model can consider at once
- Hallucination: Confident but incorrect output
- Few‑shot: Providing a few examples to guide behavior
- Chain‑of‑thought: Asking for reasoning steps
- Retrieval: Bringing in information from documents for grounding
- Guardrails: Checks and constraints to reduce errors or misuse
- Human‑in‑the‑loop: People review key steps before proceeding
