# Programs Overview

Recommended learning path for non‑technical learners:

1) Foundations → 2) Prompting → 3) Productivity → 4) AI in Business → 5) No‑Code AI → 6) Ethics & Safety → 7) Capstone

## Tracks
- Foundations: Core concepts, capabilities, limitations
- Prompting: Patterns, system design, iteration
- Productivity: Day‑to‑day workflows in docs, sheets, email, meetings
- AI in Business: Role‑based toolkits for marketing, sales, ops, HR, finance, support
- No‑Code AI: Automation with Zapier/Make, spreadsheets, forms, chatbots
- Ethics & Safety: Bias, privacy, compliance, reliability
- Capstone: Real problem, shipped solution, impact narrative

## Delivery modes
- Self‑paced modules
- Live workshops or cohort sessions
- On‑the‑job projects

## Assessment
- Entry diagnostic
- Module quizzes
- Capstone with rubric
