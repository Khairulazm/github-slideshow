# Ethics, Safety, and Compliance

Duration: 3–4 hours
Prerequisites: Foundations

## Outcomes
- Identify bias, privacy risks, and failure modes
- Apply guardrails and review processes

## Topics
- Privacy and data handling
- Bias, fairness, and representation
- Hallucinations and reliability tactics
- Legal: IP, disclosure, consent, acceptable use
- Organizational policy and audit trails

## Assessment
- Scenario analysis and mitigation plan
