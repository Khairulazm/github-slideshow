# AI in Business (Role‑Based)

Duration: 6–12 hours, mix‑and‑match by function
Prerequisites: Foundations, Prompting

## Tracks
- Marketing: audience research, briefs, ad copy, social calendar
- Sales: email outreach, objection handling, call summaries
- Operations: SOPs, checklists, quality monitoring
- HR: job descriptions, screening support, policy drafts
- Finance: memo drafting, variance analyses, dashboards
- Support: triage, knowledge surfacing, response drafting

Each track includes 3 labs mapped to real tasks.
