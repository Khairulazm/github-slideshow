# Foundations of AI (Non‑Technical)

Duration: 6–8 hours (self‑paced or 2× live sessions)
Prerequisites: None

## Outcomes
- Explain how generative AI works at a high level
- Identify strong vs weak use cases
- Understand limits, hallucinations, and safety basics

## Syllabus
- What AI can and cannot do
- Models, prompts, and context windows
- Hallucinations and verification
- Data privacy and responsible usage
- Hands‑on: Using an assistant for research and writing

## Activities
- Case study walkthroughs
- Prompt warm‑ups (rewrite, summarize, translate)
- Reflection: 10 tasks you could automate

## Assessment
- 10‑question quiz
- Mini‑assignment: Draft an AI usage guideline for your team
