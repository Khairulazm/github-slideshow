# Productivity with AI

Duration: 6–8 hours
Prerequisites: Foundations, Prompting

## Outcomes
- Automate routine writing, analysis, meeting notes, and reporting
- Integrate AI with docs, sheets, email, and calendars

## Modules
- Email drafting, tone shifting, and follow‑ups
- Document summarization and rewriting
- Spreadsheet formulas and analysis with AI assistance
- Meeting prep and notes: agendas, action items, decisions
- Knowledge search and retrieval best practices

## Lab
- Build a weekly reporting workflow that runs in <15 minutes
