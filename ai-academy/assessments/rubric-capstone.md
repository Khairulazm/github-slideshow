# Rubric: Capstone

Criteria | 1 (Emerging) | 2 (Developing) | 3 (Proficient) | 4 (Advanced)
---|---|---|---|---
Problem definition | Vague | Clear but narrow | Clear with users/risks | Clear, validated with stakeholders
Workflow design | Disjointed | Partial flow | End‑to‑end with checkpoints | Optimized, modular, documented
Prompt quality | Unstructured | Basic structure | Clear structure with examples | Robust templates and guards
Data/privacy | Unaddressed | Minimal attention | Risks identified, mitigations | Formal policy alignment and logs
Testing | Ad hoc | Few test cases | Systematic tests | Benchmarked and iterated
Impact | Unmeasured | Anecdotal | Time/quality metrics | Quantified ROI with narrative
Documentation | Minimal | Basic | Complete, reproducible | Exec‑ready with next steps
Presentation | Unclear | Understandable | Clear and engaging | Persuasive, actionable
